Kelvin Narrator – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Kelvin Narrator (the "App") does not require an account and does not collect personal data for our own purposes. All settings and content you create in the App are stored locally on your device only. To provide firmware updates and update notifications, the App contacts a small number of online services, which necessarily receive technical connection data such as your IP address. This Privacy Policy explains what this means in detail.

Kelvin Narrator was previously published by Kelvin AS Group. As of 2026, the App is published and operated by SmallRig ("we", "us", "our"). The transfer of the App does not change how it handles your data.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data through the App for our own purposes.

  • The App does not require or offer an account, login or registration.
  • The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.
  • All presets, scenes, light settings and other content you create are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers or any other server.
  • The App only connects to online services for the purposes described in section 3 (firmware updates and update checks). These requests do not contain any content you create in the App and do not include any user identifier.

If you delete the App, all locally stored App data is removed from your device.

3. Firmware updates and update checks

To keep your lights and the App up to date, the App connects to the following services when an internet connection is available:

  • Firmware information and downloads: when the App starts and when it connects to a light, it retrieves information about the latest available firmware and, if you start a firmware update, downloads the firmware file. The firmware files are currently hosted on Bitbucket, a service operated by Atlassian. Atlassian's privacy policy: https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • App update check: the App checks with the Apple App Store or Google Play whether a newer version of the App is available and, if so, shows a notice. Only public information such as the App's identifier and your store country is used for this check.

As with any internet request, the operators of these services technically receive your device's IP address and basic request information (for example time of the request and the App's request headers). This data is processed by these providers to deliver the requested files. We do not receive, combine or store this data, and the requests contain no information about you, your lights or the content you create in the App.

The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in providing safe and up-to-date firmware and App versions (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If your device is offline, the App continues to work for controlling your lights; only firmware updates and update notifications are unavailable.

4. Bluetooth, camera and device permissions

The App uses Bluetooth Low Energy to find and control Kelvin lights near you. This communication takes place directly between your device and the lights. No data from this connection is sent to us.

  • Bluetooth (iOS and Android): required to discover, connect to and control Kelvin lights and to update their firmware.
  • Nearby devices (Android 12 and later): required by the operating system for Bluetooth scanning. The App declares that it does not use Bluetooth scanning to derive your location.
  • Location (Android 11 and older only): on these Android versions the operating system requires the location permission for Bluetooth scanning. The App does not use, store or transmit your location.
  • Camera (optional): used only for the colour picker feature, which reads colours from the live camera image. The camera image is processed on your device only; no photos or videos are recorded, stored or transmitted, and no audio is accessed.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. Without Bluetooth access, the App cannot control your lights. Without camera access, only the camera colour picker is unavailable.

5. Data processed by Apple and Google

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google may process data such as download statistics and, if you have agreed to this in your device settings, crash reports and diagnostic data. This processing is carried out by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Apple and Google generally provide us only with aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not allow us to identify you. You can disable sharing of diagnostic data with app developers in your device settings at any time.
